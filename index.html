MiniResearch

Personal PubMed Reading List Builder. Search PubMed, pick the articles you want, and export them for EndNote, Zotero, or a spreadsheet.

Search terms are sent to the free NCBI PubMed database. If you use the AI query helper, what you type also goes to the AI model you choose. Do not enter patient data or other protected health information. Always verify citations against the publisher or PubMed before using them.

AI Query helper (Gemini/Anthropic)

Describe your topic in plain language. The helper suggests a PubMed query (MeSH terms, [tiab] fields, AND / OR / NOT, quotes), this page checks it against PubMed. AI can be wrong, review every query before relying on it.

AI settings (your own API key)

The key stays in this browser and is sent only to the chosen AI model. What you type in the helper is also sent to that model. Free tiers may allow the model to use submitted text to improve its products, check the provider's current terms. Do not include patient data or unpublished details your institution restricts. Do not remember your key on a shared computer.

Supports PubMed syntax: MeSH terms, [tiab] fields, AND / OR / NOT, and quotes.
